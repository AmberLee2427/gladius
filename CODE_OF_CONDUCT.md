# Code of conduct

We welcome contributors of every background and experience level. Be respectful,
assume good intent, give evidence-based technical feedback, and credit others'
work. Harassment, threats, discrimination and disclosure of private information
are unacceptable in project discussions, reviews or events.

Maintainers may moderate or remove inappropriate contributions and restrict
participation after considering the context and impact. Report concerns privately
to a maintainer through the project's hosting platform; do not post sensitive
reports publicly. A dedicated private reporting contact must be established when
the project opens to external contributors.

Adapted from the STScI package template's community guidance; see NOTICE.md and
the retained template license. This is an independent project policy.
