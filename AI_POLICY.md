# AI-assisted development

The initial package was developed with OpenAI Codex assistance at the maintainer's
request. This includes source, tests and documentation. Human maintainers remain
responsible for scientific correctness, licensing, explanations and releases.
Synthetic automated tests are evidence of behavior, not a replacement for review
of scientific assumptions or validation on representative Roman data.

Contributors should disclose substantial AI assistance in proposed changes and
be able to explain and maintain all submitted material. A human other than the
contributor should review changes before a public release. Do not submit private
data or third-party code to tools without appropriate authorization.
